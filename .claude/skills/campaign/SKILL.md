## Method

1. **Context.** Establish the brand or venture, objective, audience, funnel stage and channels. Infer what you can and ask once for the rest.
2. **KPI first.** Name the one business metric that proves success, with its baseline and target.
3. **Plan.** Use `marketing:campaign-plan` for multi-week work and `marketing:email-sequence` for email. Apply brand guidelines where they exist.
4. **Produce.** Write channel-native copy and make the matching Canva visual as one unit.
5. **Wildcard.** Include one labelled counter-intuitive option alongside the main plan.
6. **Measurement.** Say how each metric is tracked: UTMs, a tracking sheet or a connector.

## Deliver

- The brief.
- The calendar or the assets.
- The KPI and how it is tracked.
- Every assumption you made.

Never publish, schedule or send anything (Postiz, social, email) without Tom's explicit go-ahead.
