# /measure: review KPIs and outcomes

OS home: `C:\Users\tom.otieno\Desktop\OS`. Outside that folder, read `AGENTS.md` and `registry/ROUTES.md` there first.

Request: $ARGUMENTS

Skills: the `/measure` row of `registry/ROUTES.md`. For heavy data work, hand it to the `analyst` agent.

## Method

1. **Scope.** Name the venture or campaign, the goal and the period.
2. **Define each KPI.** Give its formula, data source, baseline and target. Prefer business metrics (leads, revenue, members, retention) over vanity metrics.
3. **Get the data** from a connector or file. If there is no connector, say so and propose the manual alternative, such as UTM tags or a tracking sheet.
4. **Compare** against the target and the previous period. Explain each movement with evidence, not guesses.

## Deliver

- A scorecard: KPI, current, target, trend, status.
- Three insights.
- One to three actions.

Suggest `/learn` for any lesson the numbers have now validated.
