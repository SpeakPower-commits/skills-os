## Method

1. **Set the target and lens.** If Tom named none, pick the lens where risk is highest and say why.
2. **Read only.** Change nothing during an audit.
3. **Evidence every finding** with a file and line, URL, screenshot or quote. No evidence means it is not a finding.
4. **Rate each finding** critical, high, medium or low by impact. Add the fix and a rough effort.
5. **Look for upside too.** List the missed opportunities alongside the defects.

## Deliver

- The top three findings first.
- A severity-ranked table: finding, evidence, impact, fix, effort.
- Quick wins.

Offer the fixes as a follow-up. Do not apply them unasked.
