The method

The one-page funder story

Five parts. One page. One real person or place.

1

Who

One real person, household or place. Named, with their permission.

2

The gap

What was broken, in one sentence a stranger could picture.

3

What you did

Your action, in plain verbs. Who did what?

Where the number goes
4

The evidence

One number, with its date and where it comes from.

5

The ask

What you want the funder to do next, and what it makes possible.

Then read it aloud

To someone outside your sector. Can they say who it is about, what changed and what you did?

If it needs two pages, you have two stories.
