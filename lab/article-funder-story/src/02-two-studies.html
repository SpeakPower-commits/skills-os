What the research shows

One famous experiment. One careful repeat.

2007
Small, Loewenstein and Slovic
  • People could donate real money they had earned
  • One named child, against statistics about many
  • The named child raised more
→
2023
Maier, Wong and Feldman
  • Pre-registered repeat of two of the four experiments
  • Donations were hypothetical, not real money
  • No support for the effect
Caveat: the 2023 donations were hypothetical, so it is not the last word. Sources: Small, Loewenstein and Slovic (2007), Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes 102(2). Maier, Wong and Feldman (2023), Collabra: Psychology 9(1).
