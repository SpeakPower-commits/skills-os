The 30-minute self-audit

Four checks.
No consultant required.

5min

The five-second test

Show your homepage to someone outside your sector for five seconds. Write down their words, not yours.

10min

The consistency check

Put your homepage headline, LinkedIn About line and first deck slide side by side. Circle the shared phrases.

10min

The audience check

List your three biggest follower groups. Next to them, the three you wish you reached.

5min

The one number

Pick one metric that shows the right people are listening. Record it with today's date.

30 minutes. Look again in 90 days.Which of the six is costing you most?
